<p>For students of BBS Papenburg (Germany). You need your own virtueller-stundenplan.org login.</p>

<p><strong>Features:</strong></p>
<ul>
  <li>Daily timetable with substitutions and room changes</li>
  <li>Your own homework notes with photos and reminders</li>
  <li>Optional: assignments, files and calendar events from IServ</li>
  <li>Works offline</li>
  <li>Custom names for subjects, teachers and rooms</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Privacy:</strong> No server of our own, no ads, no tracking. Your login is stored encrypted on your device and sent only to the sites you sign in to.</p>

<p><strong>Note:</strong> The app is only available in German.</p>
